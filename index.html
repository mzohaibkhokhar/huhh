Duas for Noor’s Shifa

اللَّهُمَّ رَبَّ النَّاسِ، أَذْهِبِ الْبَأْسَ، اشْفِهَا وَأَنْتَ الشَّافِي، لَا شِفَاءَ إِلَّا شِفَاؤُكَ، شِفَاءً لَا يُغَادِرُ سَقَمًا

Allāhumma Rabban-nās, adh-hibil-ba’s, ishfihā wa Antash-Shāfī, lā shifā’a illā shifā’uk, shifā’an lā yughādiru saqamā

O Allah, Lord of mankind, remove the harm and cure her. You are the Healer; there is no cure except Your cure, a cure that leaves no illness behind.

أَسْأَلُ اللَّهَ الْعَظِيمَ رَبَّ الْعَرْشِ الْعَظِيمِ أَنْ يَشْفِيَكِ

As’alullāhal-‘Azīm, Rabbal-‘arshil-‘azīm, an yashfiyaki (say 7 times)

I ask Allah the Mighty, Lord of the Mighty Throne, to cure you.

وَإِذَا مَرِضْتُ فَهُوَ يَشْفِينِ

Wa idhā maridtu fa-huwa yashfīn (Qur’an 26:80)

And when I am ill, it is He who cures me.

أَنِّي مَسَّنِيَ الضُّرُّ وَأَنتَ أَرْحَمُ الرَّاحِمِينَ

Annī massaniyad-durru wa Anta arhamur-rāhimīn (Qur’an 21:83)

Indeed, hardship has touched me, and You are the Most Merciful of the merciful.

لَا بَأْسَ طَهُورٌ إِنْ شَاءَ اللَّهُ

Lā ba’sa tahūrun in shā’ Allāh

No harm, it is a purification, if Allah wills.

حَسْبِيَ اللَّهُ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ عَلَيْهِ تَوَكَّلْتُ وَهُوَ رَبُّ الْعَرْشِ الْعَظِيمِ

Hasbiyallāhu lā ilāha illā Huwa, ‘alayhi tawakkaltu wa Huwa Rabbul-‘arshil-‘azīm

Allah is enough for me; there is no god but Him. In Him I place my trust.

Ameen. May Allah grant you complete health, strength and peace. 🤍
